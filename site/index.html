Boone County, Indiana: election results map

General-election results by precinct, as reported by the Boone County Clerk, or re-expressed on today's (2021-enacted) Indiana House, Indiana Senate and U.S. House districts. Map how a race voted, how that changed since an earlier election, turnout, or how people cast their ballots (election day, mail, early in person). Hover or tap any area to see the numbers it is built from. Secretary of State is on every midterm ballot, so it is the race to compare years without a President, Governor or U.S. Senate contest (such as 2026).
